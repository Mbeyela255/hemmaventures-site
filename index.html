ABOUT HEMMA

An active owner and long-term builder.

HEMMA Ventures Limited is a Tanzanian investment holding and venture-building company. We originate and incubate ventures, provide strategic and operating support, and allocate capital for long-term growth.

We are sector-agnostic by design. Our decisions are driven by the quality of the opportunity, the problem being solved, the economics of the business and our ability to create meaningful value.

Today, our primary focus is building ventures from within HEMMA. Over time, we intend to selectively invest in external businesses and entrepreneurs where there is strong strategic and economic alignment.

OUR APPROACH

01

Build

We identify meaningful opportunities, validate the underlying need and create businesses from the ground up where we have conviction.

02

Own & Operate

We take an active ownership approach, providing governance, strategic direction, capital allocation and shared capability while allowing each venture to build its own operating rhythm.

03

Invest

We allocate capital where we believe meaningful long-term value can be created. Initially, that capital is concentrated in ventures we originate; our mandate may expand selectively over time.

INVESTMENT PHILOSOPHY

Opportunity before sector.

We do not define ourselves by one industry. We look for compelling needs, attractive economics and credible paths to durable enterprise value.

Build where we have conviction.

Where we originate a venture, we are prepared to participate from day zero: validating the opportunity, shaping the model and assembling the capability to build it.

Invest where we can add value.

We want our involvement to contribute more than capital. Strategy, operations, finance, commercial execution and business building are part of the value we bring.

Think long term.

We are willing to build patiently. Our objective is not portfolio activity for its own sake, but businesses capable of creating enduring value.
